Captua

Captua turns a Raspberry Pi computer and two cameras into a station for photographing archival volumes, one pair of pages at a time. Through a web interface in Spanish or English, an operator captures each pair, describes the volume, and exports the images. The station works without an internet connection. Captua is open-source software, released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.

Captua is the software of the Digitization Toolkit: hardware, software and documentation with which institutions, collectives and communities with few resources can digitize their archival collections at low cost. The Toolkit builds on a decade of community-based digitization in the Global South, whose DIY book scanners depended on cheap cameras and single-board computers that are now obsolete or unavailable.

Current release: 0.2.0. It is tested on one configuration: a unit with two Canon EOS Rebel T7 cameras connected over gphoto2, and a monitor, keyboard and mouse.
